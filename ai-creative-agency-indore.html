How We Work

How Per4mance Guru Works

Four stages take an Indore brand from stale catalogue shots to a pipeline of conversion-proven visuals.

01

Audit

We score every existing visual for fatigue: click-through decay, frequency saturation and format-to-platform mismatch. Most Indore accounts run the same hero image across Meta, Google and organic without adapting aspect ratio or messaging layer.

02

Strategy

Each vertical gets a tailored production plan — recipe reels for namkeen brands, screen-capture demos for SaaS startups, packaging renders for pharma, campus snippets for education, walkthrough animations for real estate — with testing cadence mapped to seasonal calendars.

03

Launch

Initial assets reach the ad account within ten days. Every piece ships with a tracking tag linked to its visual treatment so the performance system can attribute conversions to a specific backdrop, headline overlay or video hook.

04

Optimise

Bi-weekly production sprints replace fatigued assets. Monthly creative retrospectives tie performance deltas back to design decisions — which colour palette lifted CTR, which hook held watch time — so the next brief carries real data, not guesswork.
