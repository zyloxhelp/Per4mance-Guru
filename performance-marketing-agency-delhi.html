What Not to Do

Common Mistakes Delhi Brands
Make with Digital Marketing

❌

Running Broad Campaigns Without Conversion Tracking

More than 60% of Delhi SMBs run Meta Ads without a properly configured Pixel and conversion API. The algorithm is blind — it optimises for clicks, not purchases. Set up tracking before spending a rupee.

❌

Using Only Static Images in 2026

Video content — especially short-form Reels-style ads — generates 4–6× higher thumb-stop rates than static images in Delhi's competitive feeds. Brands still running only static creative are paying 2× more for the same results.

❌

Targeting "Delhi" as a Single Audience

Delhi has 11 million households across vastly different income groups, languages, and consumer behaviours. Treating it as one audience wastes budget on irrelevant clicks. Micro-locality targeting delivers 30–50% lower CPAs.

❌

Stopping Campaigns Too Early

Meta's Advantage+ and Google's Smart Bidding need 30–60 days of data to enter their learning phase optimally. Brands that pause campaigns after 2 weeks due to early CPAs miss the compounding effect that delivers profitable scale.

❌

Ignoring AI Search Optimisation

45% of Google searches now show AI Overviews. Brands not creating content structured for AI answer extraction are losing top-of-funnel visibility. Traditional SEO alone is no longer enough for Delhi brands in 2026.

❌

Hiring a Generic Agency Without Vertical Expertise

A fashion brand in Lajpat Nagar needs a different strategy than a B2B SaaS in Noida. Agencies that apply the same playbook across verticals consistently underdeliver. Always ask for case studies from your specific industry.
