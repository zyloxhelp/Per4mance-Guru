How We Work

How Per4mance Guru Works

Four phases that convert fragmented ad accounts into a disciplined acquisition engine.

01

Audit

We examine your current accounts, check tracking implementation, review search-term reports and isolate the spend going to queries that never convert — most Pune accounts have at least twenty per cent waste hiding in broad-match terms.

02

Strategy

Each product line gets a channel recommendation, budget split and funnel map. SaaS accounts might need Google Search plus LinkedIn retargeting; a D2C label might need Meta catalogue plus email flows — the plan fits the buying cycle.

03

Launch

Campaigns go live within fourteen days of sign-off. Every ad group carries its own conversion action so early data tells us which audiences and keywords deserve more budget before the month ends.

04

Optimise

Weekly bid reviews, negative-keyword sweeps and creative refreshes keep efficiency climbing. A monthly strategy call reviews what scaled, what stalled and where the next budget increment should land.
