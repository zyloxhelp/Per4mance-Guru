How Generative Tools Solve Ahmedabad's Creative Bottleneck

The volume problem Gujarat advertisers face

An AI creative agency in Ahmedabad tackles one specific constraint: the gap between how many ad visuals paid channels consume and how quickly a production team can deliver them. Gujarat's commercial engine — garment showrooms lining CG Road, pharmaceutical plants inside Vatva GIDC, diamond polishing hubs near Vastrapur, and residential towers climbing along SG Highway — feeds an advertising machine that burns through creative assets faster than any studio can photograph, edit and deliver them. Per4mance Guru, operational since 2018 and responsible for more than one hundred crore in cumulative ad spend across 80-plus brands, closes that gap with generative tools: Midjourney for stills, Kling for short video, Runway for composited motion, and Seedance for movement-driven formats. Each asset ships into live digital marketing campaigns and earns or loses its spot based on conversion data, not creative judgement alone.

Garment and textile SKU imagery at export pace

A mid-size textile house on Ashram Road may list three thousand fabric variants across domestic and international marketplaces. Each variant needs a product card, a carousel frame and at least one short-form video for Meta catalogue campaigns. Photographing that catalogue in a traditional studio means weeks of fabric sampling, lighting setup and post-production retouching. Midjourney generates consistent flat-lay renders from reference swatches, holding weave pattern and colour fidelity across the full range in a single day. Kling then animates selected hero fabrics into looping clips that perform well in Reels and Stories placements where static imagery struggles to hold attention.

Jewellery and diamond visual commerce

Ahmedabad's jewellery market — centred around Vastrapur, Prahlad Nagar and Bodakdev — depends on imagery where every prong, bezel and facet is visible at pinch-to-zoom resolution. AI rendering delivers multi-angle product views, lifestyle compositions and 360-degree showcase clips from a single set of reference photographs. The output feeds Google Shopping listings, Instagram catalogue ads and the retailer's own product detail pages simultaneously, cutting the turnaround from collection launch to full e-commerce listing from a week to under 48 hours.

How We Work

From Brief to Scaled Ad Library

Four stages move a creative brief into a data-backed, continuously refreshed ad library.

01

Audit

We catalogue every live ad asset, flag the ones showing fatigue — rising frequency with falling CTR — and list the formats your competitors run that you currently lack.

02

Strategy

Each product line maps to a tool, a format and a testing plan. Textile SKUs get Midjourney flat-lays and Kling loops; jewellery hero pieces get Runway composited lifestyle clips.

03

The first batch of variants ships within fourteen days. Every asset carries unique tracking parameters so the scoring model receives clean conversion signals from the first impression.
