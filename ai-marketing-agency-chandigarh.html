How We Work

How Per4mance Guru Works

Four phases that turn scattered campaign data into a self-improving marketing engine.

01

Audit

We pull twelve months of ad-platform data, map conversion paths and benchmark cost-per-result against category medians to find where the budget leaks — wrong audiences, stale creatives, broken tracking.

02

Strategy

A channel plan assigns each product line or programme to the platform, format and audience segment where the algorithm predicts the strongest return, with spend gates that protect the downside.

03

Launch

Campaigns go live within two weeks. Each ad set targets a distinct audience–creative combination so performance data accrues cleanly and the algorithm learns what works without contamination.

04

Optimise

Weekly reviews feed fresh creative into the testing queue, reallocate budget toward top-performing segments and retire under-performers. Monthly strategy calls recalibrate targets against business outcomes.
