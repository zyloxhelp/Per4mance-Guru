Common Problems

Why Most Mumbai Businesses
Don't Get ROI from Digital

Across 80+ audits, the same problems keep showing up — wrong conversion events, bloated audiences, no creative rotation, zero negative keyword hygiene. Mumbai has its own version of each one.

❌
Large Agency, Junior Team
Mumbai's big agencies sell with senior partners and deliver with 2-year-olds. You pay premium rates for junior execution. Per4mance Guru puts a senior strategist on every account — not in the pitch deck, in the actual campaign.
❌
Real Estate Wasting 40% on Wrong Keywords
Mumbai real estate brands spend lakhs on broad keywords like 'property in Mumbai' — capturing renters, brokers, and researchers. The right keyword structure targets ready buyers by micro-market: 'sea-facing apartment Worli', '3 BHK Bandra West under 5 crore'.
❌
Fashion Brands: Reach Without Conversion
Mumbai fashion houses invest heavily in brand building — shoots, influencers, aesthetic feeds. But Meta Ads needs a different muscle: direct response creative, tight audience segmentation, and ROAS-tracked spend. Brand building and performance are different tools.
❌
Fintech: Meta Restrictions Killing Campaigns
Financial services brands on Meta constantly battle ad rejections, limited audience targeting, and compliance restrictions. We've navigated Meta's financial services policies across 15+ BFSI brands — knowing what works and what triggers a ban is real expertise.
