Questions founders ask us

Is there a minimum ad spend?

Yes. We work with brands spending ₹3 to 5 lakh or more a month on ads, which usually means ₹10 lakh+ in monthly sales. Below that there isn't enough data to scale reliably.

What happens if targets are missed?

We re-strategise: a creative refresh, an audience and funnel rework, and a budget reallocation. We don't promise guaranteed ROAS, and you should be wary of anyone who does.

Can you do only ads, or only creatives?

Yes. Both are available on their own, though results are strongest when one team does both.
