What social media platforms do you manage for BTM Layout businesses? +

We manage Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube, and X (Twitter). The platform mix depends on your audience. D2C brands typically prioritise Instagram and Facebook. B2B businesses get stronger results from LinkedIn. We recommend starting with two platforms done well rather than five done poorly.

How long does social media marketing take to show results? +

Organic social is a compound channel — results build over 3–6 months. Reach and engagement typically improve within 60 days as content quality improves. Revenue attribution from organic social takes longer, but brands building genuine community see meaningful referral traffic and lower CAC within 6 months.

Do you create the content for BTM Layout social media campaigns? +

Yes — end-to-end content production including strategy, scripting, direction, editing, captioning, and scheduling. You provide brand assets and final approval. For businesses with internal creative resources, we work in a hybrid model.

How many posts per month do you create for Bangalore clients? +

A typical Instagram engagement includes 12–20 posts per month (Reels, carousels, single images) plus daily Story content. LinkedIn typically runs 12–16 posts per month. We do not pad volume with low-quality content to hit a number — every post has a defined purpose.

What is the difference between organic social media and social advertising? +

Organic social (social media marketing) covers content creation, community management, and audience growth without paid promotion. Social advertising covers paid campaigns through Meta Ads Manager or LinkedIn Campaign Manager. Organic builds brand equity and community; paid drives immediate traffic and conversions. The most effective approach combines both.
