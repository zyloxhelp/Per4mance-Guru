What ROAS can I expect from Meta Ads in Bangalore? +

Our portfolio average is 3.8x ROAS. Bangalore D2C brands typically achieve 2.5x–5x ROAS at steady state. Lead generation campaigns are measured in CPL — Bangalore businesses typically see ₹200–₹800 CPL for quality leads depending on category. We give category-specific benchmarks before you commit.

How do you handle Meta Ads after iOS privacy changes? +

We implement Conversions API alongside the Meta Pixel, use aggregate event measurement, and cross-reference with Google Analytics 4 using UTM parameters. The goal is a reliable ROAS number you can make business decisions from — not a Meta-reported figure that overstates results.

Do you create the ad creatives for Indiranagar campaigns? +

Yes — end-to-end creative production is part of our scope. Scripting, direction, AI-assisted production, static design, and editing. You provide product assets; we handle creative strategy and production. For UGC-style content, we manage creator sourcing and briefing.

What is Advantage+ and should my Indiranagar business use it? +

Advantage+ Shopping is Google's AI-driven campaign type that automates targeting and creative delivery. It works well with sufficient conversion data (50+ conversions/month). For new accounts, we typically start with manual campaign structures to build data quality first. The right approach depends on your account maturity.
