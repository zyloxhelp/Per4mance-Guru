Performance Marketing Agency Questions
from T Nagar

What exactly is performance marketing? +

Performance marketing means every campaign is structured around a measurable business outcome — ROAS, CPL, or CPA. Unlike brand marketing measured in reach, performance marketing is accountable to revenue.

How is a performance marketing agency different from a regular digital agency? +

A regular digital agency measures success in engagement and reach. A performance marketing agency measures success in ROAS, CPL, and CPA — numbers that appear in your revenue report.

What channels does performance marketing cover in T Nagar? +

Performance marketing primarily covers paid channels — Meta Ads, Google Ads (Search, Shopping, YouTube), and programmatic display. The specific channel mix depends on where your T Nagar customers make purchase decisions.

What ROAS should I expect from performance marketing in Chennai? +

Our portfolio average is 3.8x ROAS. Chennai D2C brands typically achieve 2.5x–5x at steady state. We give category-specific benchmarks before you commit.

How do you measure performance marketing results? +

We use platform-native reporting, GA4, server-side event tracking, and third-party attribution. Every client gets a live dashboard with ROAS, CAC, LTV, CPL, and revenue attribution by channel.
