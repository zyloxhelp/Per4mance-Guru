Frequently Asked Questions

Performance Marketing Agency Questions from Faridabad Businesses

What exactly is performance marketing? +

Performance marketing means every campaign is structured around a measurable business outcome — typically ROAS, CPL, or CPA. Unlike brand marketing measured in reach or awareness, performance marketing is accountable to revenue. Every rupee can be traced to a specific outcome, and budget is allocated based on what is working — not what looks good in a report.

How is a performance marketing agency different from a regular digital agency? +

A regular digital agency measures success in engagement and reach. A performance marketing agency measures success in ROAS, CPL, and CPA — numbers that appear in your revenue report. The difference shows up in campaign structure (conversion-focused from day one), budget management (data-driven reallocation), and reporting (revenue attribution, not vanity metrics).

What channels does performance marketing cover in Faridabad? +

Performance marketing primarily covers paid channels — Meta Ads, Google Ads (Search, Shopping, YouTube), and programmatic display. It also incorporates SEO when measured against organic revenue contribution. The specific channel mix depends on where your Faridabad customers make purchase decisions.

How do you measure performance marketing results for Faridabad businesses? +

We use platform-native reporting, GA4, server-side event tracking, and third-party attribution. Every client gets a live dashboard with ROAS, CAC, LTV, CPL, and revenue attribution by channel. We also run incrementality tests to validate that reported performance is actually incremental.
