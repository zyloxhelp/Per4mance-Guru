What is the difference between SEO, AEO, and GEO? +

SEO gets you ranking in traditional Google blue-link results. AEO (Answer Engine Optimisation) gets you featured in Google's AI Overviews and People Also Ask boxes. GEO (Generative Engine Optimisation) gets your brand cited in ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini. All three matter in 2025 — we build strategies that target all three simultaneously.
