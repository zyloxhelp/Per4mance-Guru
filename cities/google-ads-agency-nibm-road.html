How much should I budget for Google Ads in NIBM Road? +

CPCs in NIBM Road vary by industry — typically ₹15–₹80 for commercial keywords, ₹100–₹400 for competitive niches like real estate or finance. A starting budget of ₹25,000–₹50,000/month gives enough volume to collect meaningful data. We provide CPC estimates for your specific keywords before you commit.

What is Performance Max and should NIBM Road businesses use it? +

Performance Max runs across all Google inventory — Search, Display, YouTube, Shopping, and Gmail. It works well with 50+ conversions/month and strong creative assets. For new accounts, we start with standard Search campaigns to build data quality before introducing PMax. The decision depends on account maturity, not Google's default recommendations.

How do Google Ads and SEO work together for NIBM Road businesses? +

Google Ads gives immediate visibility while SEO builds long-term organic equity. We use Ads search term data to identify high-converting keywords for the SEO strategy. Brands running both see lower effective CPC over time as organic rankings reduce paid dependency.

Do you manage Google Shopping campaigns for Pune businesses? +

Yes — Shopping is a core part of our e-commerce Google Ads work. Product feed optimisation in Google Merchant Center, Shopping campaign structure, smart bidding, and product-level performance analysis. Shopping ads typically deliver lower CPCs and higher CTRs because they show product image, price, and store name in the ad.

How long before Google Ads show results? +

Search campaigns can generate leads or sales within 24–48 hours of going live. The learning phase is 2–4 weeks. Meaningful ROAS stability comes by Month 2. By Month 3, you should have a clear picture of which campaigns and keywords are profitable.
