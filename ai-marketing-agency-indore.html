How Per4mance Guru Works

Four phases from raw account data to self-improving campaigns that strengthen their edge every week.

01

Audit

We pull twelve months of campaign data, map conversion paths, benchmark cost metrics against category medians and pinpoint the segments where AI models will deliver the fastest improvement.

02

Strategy

A channel plan ties each product line to the audience model, bid strategy and creative framework that historical data supports, with KPIs anchored to unit economics rather than vanity metrics.

03

Launch

Campaigns go live within ten days. Every ad group carries tracking parameters that isolate performance by audience, creative and placement so the AI model learns from clean, unambiguous signals.

Weekly model retraining refreshes audience segments, retires underperforming creatives and reallocates budget toward the combinations earning the strongest marginal return over time.
