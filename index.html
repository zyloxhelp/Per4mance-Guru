About Our Approach

How We Build Brands That Scale

Per4mance Guru is a performance marketing agency in Delhi built around one goal: profitable growth for every brand we work with. We manage Meta Ads and Google Ads campaigns for D2C brands, B2B companies, and direct-to-consumer startups across India, UAE, Canada, and Sri Lanka.

Unlike traditional digital marketing agencies, we combine AI-powered creative production with data-driven media buying. This means faster creative testing, lower cost-per-acquisition, and higher ROAS for our clients.

Our team of 25+ performance marketers, SEO strategists, creative directors, and AI specialists has managed over ₹100 Crore in ad spend and generated ₹1000 Crore+ in revenue for 80+ brands — from Tata Play Fiber and JJ Valaya to emerging D2C startups on Shark Tank India.

We work across Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram), Google Ads (Search, Shopping, Performance Max), SEO, content marketing, and social media — with AI creative production as our key differentiator in the market.
