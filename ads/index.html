FAQ

Questions worth answering.

What is the minimum budget to work with Per4mance Guru?

Strategy calls are open to brands spending ₹1.5 lakh or more per month on Meta or Google Ads. The systems we build only deliver meaningful returns at this level and above.

Do you work with brands outside India?

Yes. We actively manage campaigns across the UAE, US, UK, Australia, Canada, Sri Lanka, and Singapore with market-specific creative and audience strategies for each geography.

How long before we see results?

Most accounts show clear directional improvement in weeks 3 to 4. Meaningful revenue lift is visible by month 2. Hues Studio went 6x in 4 months. Timeline depends on starting point, product-market fit, and creative availability.

Do you handle creative production as well?

We build creative strategy, hooks, scripts, and briefs. We also produce AI-generated video and static creatives in-house. For UGC, we work with your existing creator network or help you build one.

What makes you different from other agencies?

We own the result, not just the execution. Our creative strategy is built on competitor review mining, real customer language, and hook banks. And we have a proven bid discipline framework that keeps CAC controlled even at aggressive scale.

Do you sign long-term contracts?

Initial engagements are 3-month minimum, followed by monthly rolling agreements. We do not lock you into 12-month contracts. We keep clients by delivering results, not by trapping them.
