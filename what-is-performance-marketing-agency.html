What Is a Performance Marketing Agency, and How Do You Choose the Right One in India?

What it does, how the money moves through a funnel, what the first 90 days look like, what it costs in India, and how to choose one.

SA
Siddhant Aneja
· Updated November 24, 2026 · 8 min read · Performance Marketing
Direct Answer

A performance marketing agency plans and runs paid campaigns, mostly on Meta and Google, and is judged on results you can count: sales, leads, cost per lead and return on ad spend (ROAS). You pay for growth you can trace back to the money you spent, not for likes, reach or followers.

I've run a performance marketing agency in Delhi since 2019, and the question founders ask me most isn't "what do you do?" It's "how do I know this will actually make me money?"

This guide answers both. It covers what these agencies do, how the money moves through a funnel, what the first 90 days look like, what it costs in India, and how to choose one. Where I quote a number, I name the source.

What Does a Performance Marketing Agency Actually Do?

It spends your ad budget where it can come back as revenue, and proves where that revenue came from. The work usually covers:

  • Meta Ads on Facebook and Instagram, for new, comparing and ready-to-buy audiences.
  • Google Ads for people already searching, through Search, Shopping and Performance Max.
  • Ad creative: static ads, Reels and video, often made faster with AI tools.
  • Tracking: GA4, the Meta Pixel and conversion events.
  • Landing pages and CRO, because a great ad sent to a slow page still loses money.

How Does Performance Marketing Actually Make Money? (The Funnel)

Every campaign is a chain: spend → clicks → landing page → purchase or lead → revenue → profit. A weak link anywhere breaks it, so a good agency works on the whole funnel, not just the ads.

A simple illustrative example, not a benchmark: ₹1,00,000 in spend brings 2,000 visitors at ₹50 a click. If 2 percent buy at ₹2,000 each, that's ₹80,000 in revenue, a ROAS of 0.8. Fix the landing page so 3 percent buy, and the same spend returns ₹1,20,000, a ROAS of 1.2.

  • Know your break-even ROAS. Divide 1 by your margin after product, shipping and payment costs. At a 50 percent margin, you need a ROAS of 2 just to break even.
  • ROAS isn't profit. Flable's 2026 report on Meta Ads profitability benchmarks says return-to-origin (RTO) shipments made up nearly 25 to 30 percent of failed orders across mid-size Indian D2C brands in 2024. The same report puts the cost of each returned COD order at ₹180 to ₹240.

How Does the Work Happen, Step by Step?

Here is our six-step process:

  1. Audit the business, competitors, ad accounts and tracking.
  2. Strategy: agree budget, audiences and targets. You approve before launch.
  3. Launch campaigns, creatives and tracking, and record starting numbers.
  4. Test and optimise weekly: hooks, audiences, landing pages.
  5. Scale slowly behind winners. See our guide to scaling a Meta Ads budget without killing ROAS.
  6. Report monthly, with next month's plan agreed in advance.

What Results Should You Expect in the First 90 Days?

Google: Google Ads Help's "About Smart Bidding learning" page says the "Learning" status "never lasts more than 7 days". It also says the system takes 1 to 2 conversion cycles to converge to a new goal.

Meta: every new or heavily edited ad set goes through a learning phase. An ad set that doesn't get enough results to finish learning shows "Learning limited" in Ads Manager. This is common when budgets are spread too thin.

How Much Does a Performance Marketing Agency Cost in India?

Agencies charge a flat retainer, a percentage of ad spend, or a hybrid. upGrowth's 2026 guide to performance marketing retainer pricing in India puts percentage-based fees at a typical 10 to 15 percent of ad spend. The same guide puts flat retainers at ₹1.5 lakh to ₹6 lakh or more a month.

SocioLabs' 2026 performance marketing pricing guide says small businesses in India typically spend ₹25,000 to ₹75,000 a month on agency fees, not counting ad spend.

We publish ours: single-channel management starts at ₹25,000 a month and full-service at ₹75,000 a month. Ad budget is separate, and we suggest around ₹50,000 a month so there is enough data to test. Unsure where to begin? Read SEO vs paid ads: where to put your first ₹1 lakh.

In-House Team vs Agency: What's the Honest Cost Comparison?

On AmbitionBox, salaries reported for Performance Marketers in India range from ₹2.2 lakh to ₹12 lakh a year for 1 to 7 years of experience. The figure is based on 601 salaries (last updated March 2025). That buys one person. Paid growth also needs design, video and tracking skills.

How Do You Choose the Right Performance Marketing Agency?

Ask these questions before you sign:

  1. Will the work happen inside my own ad accounts?
  2. Who will run my account day to day? Meet that person.
  3. Have you worked in my category?
  4. What will you report, and how often?
  5. What happens when results dip?
Red Flags

Guaranteed ROAS, ads run from the agency's account, reports with reach but no sales, and long lock-ins before results.

Key Takeaways

  • A performance marketing agency is judged on results you can count: sales, leads, cost per lead and ROAS, not reach or followers.
  • Every campaign is a chain from spend to profit. A weak landing page can lose money even when the ads are good.
  • Expect usable but noisy data in month one, improving cost per result in month two, and a repeatable picture by month three.
  • Before you sign, make sure ads run in your own accounts, meet the person who will manage them, and walk away from guaranteed ROAS.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is performance marketing the same as digital marketing?
Not quite. Digital marketing covers every online channel, including brand awareness work. Performance marketing is the part judged on results you can count, such as sales, leads, cost per lead and return on ad spend.
Which is the best performance marketing agency in Delhi?
There is no single best agency for every business. Pick one that has worked with brands like yours, runs ads in your own accounts and reports on sales, not reach. Per4mance Guru is one Delhi option, near GTB Nagar metro since 2019.
How do I find a performance marketing agency near me?
Search Google Maps, read recent reviews and shortlist three agencies. Then meet the person who will actually run your account. Who manages your ads matters more than the distance to their office.
Who is the best performance marketing agency in India?
Lists that crown one "best" agency are usually adverts. Judge on fit: category experience, comfort with your budget, your own ad accounts and honest weekly reporting. Ask three agencies for a free audit and compare their thinking.
Should I hire a local agency in Gurgaon, Noida or Mumbai?
Not necessarily. Ad accounts, tracking and reports all live online, so a good agency in any city can run your campaigns. Choose on category experience and reporting quality, and plan in-person reviews if you want them.
How much does a performance marketing agency charge in India?
upGrowth's 2026 guide puts percentage fees at 10 to 15 percent of ad spend. SocioLabs' 2026 guide says small businesses typically spend ₹25,000 to ₹75,000 a month on agency fees. Per4mance Guru starts at ₹25,000 (single channel) and ₹75,000 (full service) a month.
How long does performance marketing take to show results?
Expect early data within days and steadier results over two to three months. Google Ads Help says the Smart Bidding "Learning" status never lasts more than 7 days. It also says the system takes 1 to 2 conversion cycles to converge to a new goal.
