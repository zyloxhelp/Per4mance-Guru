Performance Marketing Services in India, Built Around Revenue

What are performance marketing services?

Performance marketing services in India are marketing services where the agency is measured on business outcomes such as sales, qualified leads and return on ad spend, not impressions or reach. Per4mance Guru delivers performance marketing services in India across 9 core disciplines, from Meta Ads and Google Ads to SEO, AI creative and analytics, for 80+ brands at a 3.8x average ROAS. It is the branch of digital marketing where every campaign carries a cost per result.

Which channel should a brand start with?

The right starting channel depends on how your customers buy. D2C and e-commerce brands usually start on Meta Ads, where Advantage+ shopping campaigns and Reels Ads create demand at scale. Service businesses, B2B companies and high-intent categories such as education and real estate usually start on Google Ads Search, because buyers are already looking. SEO and content compound over 3–6 months and lower blended acquisition costs once paid channels are profitable.

How are services packaged and priced?

Per4mance Guru does not sell fixed bundles. Each engagement is scoped after a free audit, with ad spend and management fees shown separately, so you always see what goes to Meta or Google and what goes to the agency. Most brands start with one or two channels and add SEO, content or AI creative once the core paid accounts are stable. See how this works for a performance marketing agency in India across four markets, or locally as a performance marketing agency in Delhi and a digital marketing agency in Laxmi Nagar.

How does Per4mance Guru measure results?

When we audit a new account, the biggest leak is usually measurement, not media. Default Meta pixel reporting over-credits view-through conversions, and Google Ads accounts often spend on search terms nobody has reviewed in months. We fix tracking with the Conversions API and GA4, cut wasted queries, and only then scale with Performance Max and Advantage+. In one Bangalore B2B SaaS account, the first search term audit found 40% of spend going to irrelevant queries.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What do performance marketing services in India include?

Performance marketing services in India usually cover paid media on Meta Ads and Google Ads, conversion tracking, creative production and reporting, with SEO and content added for long-term growth. At Per4mance Guru, that means 9 services under one team, from Performance Max and Advantage+ campaigns to AI video and GA4 attribution, all measured against ROAS, CPL or CPA targets.

How much does performance marketing cost in India?

You pay for two things: media and management. Meta Ads needs roughly ₹25,000–₹30,000 a month to give the algorithm enough data, and Google Ads ₹25,000–₹50,000 depending on cost per click in your category. Management is typically 12–18% of ad spend with a monthly minimum. A one-time technical SEO audit starts from ₹8,000, and local SEO from ₹15,000 a month.

How is Per4mance Guru different from other agencies?

Most agencies split paid media, creative and SEO across different teams or vendors, and attribution gets lost between them. Per4mance Guru keeps all nine services under one senior-led team, produces creative in-house with AI tools, and connects every channel to revenue through Conversions API and GA4. Contracts start with a three-month minimum and agreed KPIs, not long lock-ins.

What results can a brand expect in 90 days?

Paid channels move first. Campaigns go live in week two after onboarding, early data arrives within 7–14 days, and Meta Ads and Google Ads usually stabilise within 30–60 days. SEO and content take longer, typically 3–6 months to rank. A South Delhi education brand cut its Google Ads cost per lead by 58% in the first month after we fixed its conversion tracking.

Do you work with brands outside Delhi?

Yes. Per4mance Guru delivers its services to brands across India and in the UAE, Sri Lanka and Canada from its Delhi office at GT Karnal Industrial Area. Audits, onboarding, weekly optimisation calls and monthly reports all run remotely, and campaigns can target one city, several states or the whole country, depending on where your customers are.
