What is a good ROAS for Indian D2C brands in 2026?
A "good" ROAS depends entirely on your gross margins. A brand with 60% gross margin can be profitable at 1.8x ROAS. A brand with 30% gross margin needs 4x+ ROAS to cover costs. Use this calculator with your actual COGS to find your break-even ROAS — that number matters more than any industry benchmark.
Why is my "true ROAS" lower than my reported ROAS?
Meta's default attribution includes view-through conversions — sales where the customer saw your ad but did not click it. These are often organic conversions that Meta is taking credit for. The view-through inflation slider adjusts for this. To fix it properly, change your attribution window in Meta Ads Manager from "7-day click + 1-day view" to "7-day click only."
What is break-even ROAS and how is it calculated?
Break-even ROAS is the ROAS at which your ad revenue exactly covers your total costs (COGS + shipping + ad spend + management fees). Formula: Break-even ROAS = 1 ÷ (1 - COGS% - Shipping%). If your COGS is 35% and shipping is 8%, your break-even ROAS = 1 ÷ (1 - 0.35 - 0.08) = 1 ÷ 0.57 = 1.75x. Any ROAS above this is profitable; any below it loses money.
How much should I spend on Meta Ads vs Google Ads?
For most Indian D2C brands, a 60-70% Meta / 30-40% Google split works well. Meta is better for awareness, discovery, and upper-funnel demand generation. Google captures existing demand from users actively searching. Brands with strong brand awareness should shift more budget to Google; new brands should weight Meta more heavily initially.
