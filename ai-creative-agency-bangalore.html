Why Startups Choose an AI Creative Agency in Bangalore

Why do Bangalore startups need more creative than a traditional agency makes?

Per4mance Guru works as an AI creative agency in Bangalore for SaaS companies, D2C tech brands and funded startups that use up ad creative faster than a monthly shoot can replace it. When a funding round lands, ad budgets can rise sharply within weeks, and Meta's Advantage+ campaigns need a steady supply of new hooks and formats to spend that money well. AI production with Kling, Seedance, Runway and Midjourney closes that gap. It is one part of digital marketing, but for a startup scaling paid social it is usually the bottleneck.

How do SaaS companies turn their product into ads?

SaaS products are hard to film and easy to show. We build demo and explainer videos from screen recordings, product UI and short scripts, then use AI tools for motion, backgrounds and visual polish, so a new feature can become an ad in days rather than after a full production cycle. Each video is cut into several versions with different opening hooks, such as the problem, the outcome or a customer quote, because the first two seconds decide whether a B2B buyer keeps watching.

What works for D2C tech and consumer-app brands?

Bangalore D2C tech and consumer brands tend to win with ads that feel native to the feed: UGC-style reviews, unboxing clips, quick product comparisons and founder-style videos that explain why the product exists. AI production lets us make many of these without booking creators for every variation. We then test them inside Meta Ads Manager in structured batches, keep the angles that lower cost per purchase and retire the rest, rather than letting a single hero video run until it fatigues.

How do we work with Bangalore teams from Delhi?
