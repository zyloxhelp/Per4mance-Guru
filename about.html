Per4mance Guru: A Performance Marketing Agency in India Built on Revenue

Who is Per4mance Guru?

Per4mance Guru is a performance marketing agency in India that ties Meta Ads, Google Ads and SEO spend to revenue, not reach. Founded in Delhi in 2018, the agency has scaled 80+ brands and managed ₹100 crore+ in ad spend at a 3.8x average ROAS. Unlike a traditional marketing agency that is paid for activity, Per4mance Guru is judged on sales, qualified leads and return on ad spend.

Where is Per4mance Guru based, and which markets does it serve?

Per4mance Guru works from B-41, 3rd Floor, GT Karnal Industrial Area, Delhi 110033, and runs campaigns for brands across India, the UAE, Sri Lanka and Canada. Clients range from D2C startups and luxury fashion labels to education, wellness, real estate and B2B service companies, including enterprise accounts such as Tata Play and HRX. Working across four markets means our strategists see how the same product, budget and creative perform differently in Delhi, Dubai and Toronto, and we apply those lessons to every Indian account.

Who runs the accounts?

Co-founders Siddhant Aneja and Anchal Mehta lead a team of 35+ specialists, including senior media buyers, Google Ads specialists, Meta Ads specialists and an SEO head. Senior strategists own every account from the first audit, so the people who plan your campaigns are the same people who run them in Meta Ads Manager and Google Ads every day.

How does Per4mance Guru measure success?

In almost every account we take over, the first fix is measurement, not media. We set up the Conversions API, GA4 and clean conversion events before touching budgets, then scale with Advantage+ campaigns, Performance Max and Reels Ads once the data is reliable. That is how one Delhi NCR fashion brand went from 1.8x to 4.2x ROAS in 12 weeks. Our Story

Built for Performance.

Per4mance Guru didn't start in a glass-walled office with a ping-pong table. It started with a genuine frustration — watching great brands waste money on agencies that chased vanity metrics, produced beautiful-looking reports, and delivered zero actual growth.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a performance marketing agency in India do?

A performance marketing agency in India manages paid and organic channels, such as Meta Ads, Google Ads, Performance Max and SEO, and is paid to deliver measurable outcomes like sales, leads and ROAS. Per4mance Guru sets up Conversions API and GA4 tracking first, then builds campaigns, produces creative and reports monthly on revenue, not impressions or follower counts.

How much does performance marketing cost in India?

Costs split into ad spend and management. Most Indian brands need at least ₹25,000–₹30,000 a month on Meta Ads and ₹25,000–₹50,000 on Google Ads for the algorithms to learn. Full-service engagements covering Meta, Google and SEO typically run ₹75,000–₹2,50,000 a month all-in. Per4mance Guru prices each brand after a free audit, with KPIs agreed upfront.

How is Per4mance Guru different from other agencies?

Three things set Per4mance Guru apart. Senior strategists, led by co-founders Siddhant Aneja and Anchal Mehta, run every account instead of junior executives. Creative is produced in-house with AI tools like Kling, Runway and Midjourney, so testing is fast and affordable. And every report connects spend to revenue through Conversions API and GA4, not to reach or engagement.

What results can a brand expect in 90 days?

Within 90 days, most brands move from setup to stable performance. Tracking and account restructuring happen in weeks one and two, early data arrives within 7–14 days, and Meta Ads and Google Ads typically stabilise within 30–60 days. A Bangalore B2B SaaS client cut cost per lead from ₹8,000 to ₹3,200 within 60 days on the same budget.

Do you work with brands outside Delhi?

Yes. Per4mance Guru is based in Delhi but works with brands across India and in the UAE, Sri Lanka and Canada. Most of our collaboration, including audits, strategy calls, creative reviews and monthly reporting, happens remotely, and our multi-market experience helps brands that sell nationally or to diaspora audiences adapt creative and targeting for each region.
